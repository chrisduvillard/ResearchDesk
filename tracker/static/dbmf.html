How to read the exposure

Signed notional value ÷ total fund net assets × 100. Exposures can exceed 100% and do not measure risk contribution. Gross exposure is calculated before opposing contracts are netted. Exposure changes do not establish actual trades.

Bond direction refers to bond prices. Currency direction refers to the named currency against the US dollar. Historical reports are observations on their stated dates; positions between reports are unknown. ETF proxies and futures references may differ from the contracts DBMF held. No portfolio return is estimated.
